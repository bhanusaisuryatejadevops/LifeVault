# Google Play Store Screenshot Specifications

Google Play Console requires at least **2 phone screenshots** (maximum of 8 per form factor). High-performing store listings use 5–7 portrait screenshots that walk potential users through the actual app experience.

---

## Technical Specifications for Google Play

- **Format:** JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
- **Minimum dimension:** 320 px.
- **Maximum dimension:** 3840 px.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (portrait standard for phone listings).
- **Recommended Phone Resolutions:**
  - `1080 × 2400 px` (Standard 20:9 modern Android device)
  - `1080 × 2340 px` (Standard 19.5:9 modern Android device)
  - `1080 × 1920 px` (Standard 16:9 Android device)
- **Max File Size:** Up to 8 MB per screenshot.
