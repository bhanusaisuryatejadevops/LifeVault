# Step-by-Step Screenshot Capture Guide

Since LifeVault is installed on your physical Android device, capturing genuine screenshots directly on your phone ensures 100% authentic, high-resolution visuals that Google Play reviewers and potential users trust.

---

## Preparation Before Capturing (2 Minutes)

1. **Clean Phone Status Bar (Optional, Professional Polish):**
   - On Android: Go to **Settings > System > Developer options > Demo mode** (or System UI Tuner).
   - Turn on **Show demo mode** to set the clock to 9:00/10:00, full Wi-Fi, and 100% battery icon for pristine screenshots.
2. **Add 3 to 4 Realistic Records into LifeVault:**
   To make screenshots engaging and relatable to everyday consumers, enter 3–4 realistic items into the app:
   - **Item 1 (Urgent Return):** `Sony WH-1000XM5 Headphones`, Category: Electronics, Price: $398.00, Return Deadline: 2 days from today, Warranty: 1 year.
   - **Item 2 (Active Coverage):** `MacBook Pro 16"`, Category: Electronics, Price: $2,499.00, Warranty: 2 years, attach a sample receipt photo.
   - **Item 3 (Document Renewal):** `International Passport`, Category: Documents & IDs, Document Expiry: 6 months from today.
   - **Item 4 (Expiring Soon):** `Dyson Cordless Vacuum`, Category: Appliances, Price: $549.00, Warranty: 14 days from today.

---

## The 6 Specific Screenshots to Capture

### Screenshot 1: Dashboard (Hero Screen)
- **Navigation:** Open the app and tap **Dashboard** tab.
- **What should be visible:**
  - "Total Protected Assets" card showing the cumulative value (e.g., $3,446.00).
  - "Active Coverage", "Urgent Returns", "Expiring Soon" metric cards.
  - Quick action buttons ("Add" and "Scan").
  - Upcoming Deadlines list.
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down` on your Android device.

### Screenshot 2: Vault Inventory (Search & Categories)
- **Navigation:** Tap **Vault** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
- **What should be visible:**
  - Search bar with clear category filter chips ("Electronics", "Appliances", "Home", etc.).
  - Status filter chips ("Return Window Closing", "Active Coverage").
  - List of item cards with status badges and countdown counters.
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down`.

### Screenshot 3: Record Details View
- **Navigation:** Tap on the `MacBook Pro` or `Sony Headphones` card from the Vault list.
- **What should be visible:**
  - High-resolution attached receipt preview card with "Tap to enlarge".
  - Status badge (e.g. "Active Coverage" or "2 days to return").
  - Formatted price, purchase date, warranty expiration date.
  - Serial number with the copy button.
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down`.

### Screenshot 4: On-Device Receipt Scanner & OCR Review
- **Navigation:** Tap the `+` button, tap **Attach Photo** or pick a receipt, then tap **Run OCR**.
- **What should be visible:**
  - The bottom sheet `Review OCR Extraction` modal open over the form.
  - Candidate chips for Store/Merchant (e.g. "Best Buy"), Candidate Total Price (e.g. "$398.00"), and Candidate Purchase Date.
  - Shows potential users that the app does intelligent on-device parsing with human verification.
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down`.

### Screenshot 5: Timeline & Schedule
- **Navigation:** Tap **Timeline** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
- **What should be visible:**
  - Chronological schedule organized into "Upcoming Deadlines".
  - Color-coded badges indicating Return Window (Amber), Warranty Expiry (Navy/Teal), and Document Renewal (Green).
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down`.

### Screenshot 6: Settings & Privacy
- **Navigation:** Tap **Settings** tab.
- **What should be visible:**
  - The highlighted **100% On-Device Privacy** card.
  - Smart Reminder Schedule detailing 2-day return and 14-day warranty alerts.
  - Backup & Data Export actions ("Export Encrypted Backup").
- **How to capture:** Press `Power + Volume Down`.

---

## Transferring to Your Computer

1. Connect your phone to your PC via USB (or send via Google Drive, Quick Share, or email).
2. Copy the 6 screenshots into the `LifeVault_PlayStore_Package/Screenshots/` folder on your PC.
3. They are immediately ready for upload to Google Play Console under **Store presence > Main store listing > Phone screenshots**.
