## 2. Visual Identity & Brand Design System

LifeVault's visual identity reflects trust, privacy, longevity, and financial clarity:

### Color Palette

- **Vault Navy (Primary / Identity):** `#1B2A4A` (Background & Branding) / `#1E3A8A` (Primary interactive)
  - Conveys bank-grade security, digital safe, and personal confidentiality.
- **Teal / Sage (Secondary / Warranty):** `#0F766E` / `#14B8A6`
  - Symbolizes active protection, verified certificates, and growth.
- **Amber / Gold (Urgency / Alert):** `#D97706` / `#F59E0B`
  - Alerts users to closing return windows and expiring coverage.
- **Coral Red (Expired / Attention):** `#EF4444` / `#E11D48`
  - Clearly denotes elapsed coverage without causing anxiety.
- **Slate Crisp Neutral (Surface & Contrast):** `#F8FAFC` (Light) / `#0B1120` & `#1E293B` (Dark)
  - High-contrast, clean backdrop ensuring effortless legibility.

### Iconography Principles

- **Vault Shield with Central Lock & Document:** Represents on-device encryption, protected receipts, and personal guarantees.
- **No Third-Party Logos:** The branding strictly avoids protected Google, Android, or third-party trademarks, preventing policy strikes during Play Store automated review.
- **Clean Silhouettes:** Free of tiny distracting text, ensuring high legibility even as a small 48dp app icon on low-density mobile displays.
