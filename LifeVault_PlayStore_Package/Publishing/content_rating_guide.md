# Google Play IARC Content Rating Questionnaire Guide

In Google Play Console, navigate to:
**Policy and programs > App content > Content ratings**

Click **Start questionnaire**. Fill in your contact email and follow these verified answers for LifeVault:

---

## 1. Category Selection
- **Select Category:** Choose **Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other**.

---

## 2. Questionnaire Responses

| Question | Answer | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Does the app contain violence?** | **No** | Offline organizer with zero violent content. |
| **Does the app contain fear or horror elements?** | **No** | None. |
| **Does the app contain sexual content or nudity?** | **No** | None. |
| **Does the app contain profanity or crude humor?** | **No** | None. |
| **Does the app contain controlled substances (drugs/alcohol/tobacco)?** | **No** | None. |
| **Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with other users (chat/messaging)?** | **No** | LifeVault is 100% device-local with no multiplayer or user-to-user chat. |
| **Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users?** | **No** | No location sharing. |
| **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods?** | **No** | No in-app purchases or subscriptions. |
| **Does the app contain simulated gambling?** | **No** | None. |
| **Does the app contain news or political content?** | **No** | None. |

---

## 3. Summary & Expected Ratings

After completing the questionnaire, click **Save** and **Calculate rating**.

You will receive the following international ratings:
- **ESRB (North America):** `Everyone (E)`
- **PEGI (Europe):** `PEGI 3`
- **USK (Germany):** `USK 0`
- **Google Play:** `Rated for 3+`

Click **Submit rating** to apply this certificate to your store listing.
