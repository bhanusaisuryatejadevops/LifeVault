# Developer Contact & Support Details for Google Play Store

In Google Play Console, navigate to:
**Grow > Store presence > Store settings > Store listing contact details**

Fill in the following fields with your actual details:

1. Email Address (Mandatory):
   support@yourdomain.com
   [REPLACE with your real public support email address, e.g. yourname.apps@gmail.com]

2. Phone Number (Optional):
   [Leave blank unless you offer phone-based customer support]

3. Website URL (Optional / Recommended):
   https://YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME.github.io/lifevault-privacy/
   [Can point to your free GitHub Pages privacy website or personal portfolio]

4. Physical Address (Required by Google Play if Monetization/Ads are enabled):
   [Google Play policy requires developers whose apps display ads or in-app purchases to provide a physical mailing address in Play Console account settings for consumer protection compliance.]
   Street Address: [Your Mailing Address / P.O. Box]
   City, State/Province: [Your City, State]
   Country: [Your Country]
   Postal Code: [Your Postal Code]
