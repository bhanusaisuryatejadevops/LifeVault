# Google Play Categorization, Tags, and Keywords

## 1. App Categorization
In Google Play Console under **Store presence > Store settings**:
- **Application type:** App
- **Category:** `Productivity` (Recommended) or `Tools`
- **Secondary classification (if prompted):** `House & Home` or `Finance`

---

## 2. Store Tags
Select up to 5 relevant tags in Play Console:
1. `Productivity`
2. `Tools`
3. `Organization`
4. `Utilities`
5. `Document management`

---

## 3. Alternative Title Options (All ≤ 30 Characters)

Google Play enforces a strict 30-character limit for app titles:

| Option | Title String | Character Count | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Option 1 (Primary)** | `LifeVault: Private Organizer` | 28 / 30 | Best overall brand & utility blend |
| **Option 2** | `LifeVault: Warranty Tracker` | 27 / 30 | Best keyword focus on warranties |
| **Option 3** | `LifeVault: Receipts & Warranty` | 30 / 30 | Maximum keyword density |
| **Option 4** | `LifeVault: Offline Organizer` | 28 / 30 | Emphasizes privacy & offline strength |

---

## 4. Search Keywords & ASO (App Store Optimization) Strategy

Integrate these organic search keywords naturally into your store listing, update notes, and marketing communications:

### High-Intent Core Keywords
- warranty tracker, warranty organizer, product warranty manager
- receipt keeper, receipt scanner offline, purchase receipts
- return deadline reminder, return window tracker
- document expiration, passport renewal reminder, license expiry tracker
- offline organizer, private document vault, secure personal inventory

### Semantic Keywords
- asset tagger, serial number tracker, electronics warranty, appliance warranty
- home inventory manager, proof of purchase organizer, local reminder alarms
